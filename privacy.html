Synora Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Synora handles information when you use the Synora mobile application.

Information processed by the app

Synora collects information only when you actively use a feature that requires it. Microphone audio is collected when you start recording. Speech transcripts and text are collected when you request translation, an AI answer, or a summary. Text from TXT, Markdown, or PDF files is collected when you intentionally select a file and use it as AI context. The app also processes language choices, app settings, your Groq API credential, and service-usage information necessary to provide its features.

How information is used

This information is used to transcribe speech, translate text, answer questions, create summaries, import context, display service limits, and operate and improve the reliability of the requested features. Synora does not use this information for advertising and does not sell personal information.

Third-party services

Synora sends audio, transcripts, prompts, AI context, and related request metadata to Groq when you use Groq-powered transcription or AI features. These requests use the Groq API key that you provide. Groq states that it retains usage metadata and may temporarily retain inputs and outputs for system reliability and abuse monitoring. See Your Data in GroqCloud and the Groq Privacy Policy.

Text submitted for translation may be sent to Google translation services. Google's handling of information is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Speech recognition initiated through Apple services is subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.

Before Synora sends personal data to Groq or Google, the app displays a notice that identifies each provider, the categories of data sent, and the purposes of the transfer. No such transfer occurs unless you choose “Allow and Continue.” You can decline without providing consent and can revoke consent later in the app's settings.

Service-provider protections

Groq and Google process information only to deliver the services requested through Synora and under their published contractual, privacy, retention, and security commitments. We require service providers used by Synora to maintain protections for personal information that are the same as or equivalent to the protections described in this policy. Synora does not authorize these providers to use app data for advertising, cross-app tracking, or sale to data brokers.

Storage on your device

App preferences and the Groq API key you enter are stored locally on your device. Audio segments used for transcription are handled as temporary app files. Synora does not operate its own server or user-account system. Removing the app deletes data stored in the app's local container, subject to the behavior of your device backups.

Data retention and deletion

Synora retains local information until you change it, clear it, or remove the app. Third-party services retain information according to their own policies and account settings. You can manage Groq data controls, including eligible zero-data-retention settings, through your Groq account.

Your choices

You control microphone, speech-recognition, and photo-library permissions in iOS Settings. You choose when to record audio, select a file, or submit content to an online service. You can remove your Groq API key in Synora's settings and can stop using the online features at any time.

Children

Synora is not directed specifically to children and does not knowingly collect children's personal information for advertising or profiling.

Security and international processing

Synora uses encrypted HTTPS connections when communicating with online services. Third-party providers may process information in countries other than your own, as described in their policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Synora or applicable requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are published.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email developer@synora.app.
