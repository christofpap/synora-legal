Synora Support

Need help with Synora? Email developer@synora.app.

Before contacting support

  • Confirm that microphone and speech-recognition access are enabled in iOS Settings.
  • Check your internet connection for translation and AI-assisted features.
  • Verify that your Groq API key is valid and entered correctly in Synora settings.
  • Include your iOS version and a short description of the issue in your email.
